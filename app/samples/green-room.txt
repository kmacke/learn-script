THE GREEN ROOM
A short rehearsal scene

CHARACTERS
MAYA - an anxious lead
JULES - a patient stage manager
DIRECTOR - offstage voice

SCENE 1. Backstage, five minutes to places.

JULES
Places in five, Maya. You good?

MAYA
I forgot the second-act speech. The whole thing just—gone.

JULES
You haven't forgotten it. You're just listening to the fear instead of the line.

MAYA
Easy for you to say. You don't have to walk out there and be someone's heartbreak.

JULES
True. I just have to keep the heartbreak on schedule.

[A knock. The DIRECTOR calls from the hall.]

DIRECTOR
Maya? If you're going to throw up, do it downstage of the curtain this time.

MAYA
I'm not going to throw up.

JULES
That's the spirit. Cue-to-cue. I'll give you the line before.

MAYA
"I would have waited." That's the first one.

JULES
And the rest will be standing behind it, in order, like they always are.

MAYA
Don't let me skip the pause after "waited."

JULES
I won't. That's the whole play, that pause.

[The stage lights bump up. Distant music.]

DIRECTOR
Places!

MAYA
Okay. Okay. I would have waited.

JULES
Go.

SCENE 2. The same, after the scene.

MAYA
I remembered.

JULES
You always do.

MAYA
Don't sound so surprised.

JULES
I'm not surprised. I'm on time.
